name: Thesis performance claims
description: Distinguishes reported thesis results from measurements of this attendance app.
---

Do not present thesis-reported accuracy, latency, satisfaction, or time-savings figures as measured results for this application unless the app has been evaluated with evidence.

**Why:** A comparison with a thesis can establish feature alignment, but it does not validate the thesis's experimental results for a different implementation.

**How to apply:** When describing this app or its thesis alignment, label thesis figures as reported results and state that they have not been independently verified here.
